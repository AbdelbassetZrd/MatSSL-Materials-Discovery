\section{Results Comparison on Stability}

Thermodynamic stability (Alexandria, \StabN{} compositions, \StabPos{}\% within
0.05\,eV/atom of the convex hull) is the largest and the hardest of the three SSL tasks. The
protocol is the same: 1,000 labels and 3 seeds; the test set is capped at 20,000 held-out
compositions.

\subsection*{Comparison of SSL Methods}

\resfig{\textwidth}{results/stability/gnome/stability_methods_comparison_balanced.png}{Comparison
of SSL methods on the Stability dataset (unlabeled pool: GNoME).}

\maybeinput{table_stability_gnome}

With the GNoME pool, \StabGnBestMethod{} (\texttt{\StabGnBestConfig}) reaches \StabGnBestComp{}
against \StabGnBaseComp{} for the tuned \StabGnBaseConfig{} baseline (\StabGnImpr\%), within
one standard deviation. Self-Training is tied with the baseline. Label Propagation and Label
Spreading stay below 0.59 (ROC-AUC $\approx$ 0.70). Compositions with the same elements can be stable or unstable depending on their
stoichiometry and structure, so neighbouring points in descriptor space often carry different
labels, and propagating labels along the graph fails. With the same-database pool, the best
SSL method is \StabInBestSSL{} with \StabInBestSSLComp{} (\StabInImpr\%).

\subsection*{Effect of the Number of Unlabeled Compositions}

\resfig{\textwidth}{results/stability/gnome/stability_learning_curves.png}{Composite score and
F1-score versus the number of unlabeled compositions (stability, GNoME pool).}

\maybeinput{poolsize_stability_gnome}
\maybeinput{poolsize_stability_indist}

The degradation with the pool size is strongest on stability. Co-Training falls from 0.684
with 1,000 GNoME compositions to 0.525 with 100,000, and Self-Training from 0.682 to 0.615.
A likely explanation is that GNoME was built from compositions the generative search predicted
to be stable, so its distribution is very different from Alexandria's. As the pool grows, the
pseudo-labels on these compositions increasingly outweigh the 1,000 true labels.

\resfig{0.8\textwidth}{results/stability/gnome/stability_label_budget_curve.png}{Label-budget

The baseline gains 0.13 composite points between 250 and 5,000 labels, and the fully
supervised model trained on all 349,432 training labels reaches \StabGnFullComp{} (ROC-AUC
\StabGnFullAUC{}). Even with every label, composition alone cannot decide stability, which
also depends on the crystal structure and on the competing phases. With 250 labels, the
pseudo-labels of Co-Training hurt (0.570 against 0.603); from 2,500 labels on, the two curves
are equivalent.

\subsection*{Selected Model}

Re-trained on all training labels plus the unlabeled pool, the deployed model reaches a
composite score of \StabSelComp{} on the held-out test set (ROC-AUC \StabSelAUC{}, F1
\StabSelFone{}, recall \StabSelRec{}). Its unlabeled pool was chosen by the deployment
composite score (\StabSelPool{}).

Consequently, \textbf{\StabSelMethod{}} (\texttt{\StabSelConfig}) is selected as the primary
architecture for the deployment phase of the stability prediction module.
